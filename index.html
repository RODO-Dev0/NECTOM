Hardware

Requires a real external audio interface plus transducers actually rated for 20–60kHz — a phone or laptop's built-in mic/speaker physically cannot do this, no matter what's set here. The app checks the sample rate it actually gets and will refuse to run rather than fail silently.

How it works

Each character is sent as a pair of short tones (16 possible pitches per tone, one pitch per hex digit). A checksum rides along at the end so the receiver can tell you if anything came through corrupted, rather than silently guessing.

Privacy Policy

This page has no server, no account, and no backend of any kind — everything runs entirely inside your own browser tab.

Microphone. When you press Start Listening, audio is analyzed and, in Voice mode, recorded — all locally, in your browser's memory. Nothing is uploaded anywhere or sent to RODO_Dev. Closing the tab discards it, unless you explicitly use a Save/Download button, which saves to your own device the normal way.

Images, text, and voice clips you send. Processed entirely on-device to build the audio that plays out of your speaker. None of it is transmitted anywhere except as literal sound, through the air, by your own action.
