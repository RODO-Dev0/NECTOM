Terms of Service

This tool is provided free, as-is, with no warranty of any kind. It's an experimental way to move a picture, a short message, or a voice note through the air as sound — treat it as a demo, not a dependable communications channel.

No guarantee of accuracy. Acoustic transmission is inherently lossy: background noise, device hardware, and room acoustics can all corrupt or misalign what comes through. Don't rely on it for anything where a wrong or missing character, pixel, or word would matter.

Encryption covers Text mode only. When a secret code is active, text messages are encrypted with AES-256-GCM before transmission. Image and voice content are never encrypted — they use continuous, lossy encodings that need bit-perfect data to decrypt at all, which a noisy mic/speaker path can't reliably guarantee. Encrypting them would silently make them undecodable far more often than plaintext would, so it isn't offered.

The code fingerprint is not proof of identity. It confirms both sides typed the same secret code — nothing more. Anyone who learns your code can produce the same fingerprint. Compare fingerprints over a channel you already trust, and don't treat a name someone enters in this app as verified.

Privacy Policy

This page has no server, no account, and no backend of any kind — everything runs entirely inside your own browser tab.

Your profile and friends list. Your name, location, profile photo, and secret code — and any friends and their codes you save — are stored only in this browser's local storage on this device. None of it is transmitted anywhere, including your profile photo, which is never sent over sound. Use Settings to clear this data at any time.

Your secret code is stored in plain form locally for convenience, and is used only to derive an on-device encryption key (PBKDF2 with a fixed, application-wide salt — not a per-user random one, disclosed here as a real limitation: it gives no extra protection against someone guessing a common or short code). Pick a long or genuinely random code if the encryption matters to you. The code itself is never transmitted, only ciphertext it produces.

Microphone. When you press Start Listening, audio is analyzed and, in Voice mode, recorded — all locally, in your browser's memory. Nothing is uploaded anywhere or sent to RODO_Dev. Closing the tab discards it, unless you explicitly use a Save/Download button, which saves to your own device the normal way.

Images, text, and voice clips you send. Processed entirely on-device to build the audio that plays out of your speaker. None of it is transmitted anywhere except as literal sound, through the air, by your own action.

No tracking. No cookies, no analytics, no third-party scripts, no fonts or resources loaded from anywhere else — the page doesn't make network requests with your content, full stop.
